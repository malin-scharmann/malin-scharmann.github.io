About Me

I graduated from Utah State University with a Bachelors of Science majoring in Human Experience Design and Interaction (HEDI) with Emphases in Product Development and Multimedia. Additionally, I am minoring in the Mandarin Chinese Language.

My experience working as a Research Assistant for Project UNITE has given me the amazing opportunity to work more closely with individuals within the Instructional Technology and Learning Sciences (ITLS) department. I have learned a lot about writing and data analysis for research-based studies, as well as co-designing strategies of collaboration and communication. My involvement within my department extends to being the Instructional Technology Student Association (ITSA) Vice President of Undergrad and the Undergraduate Teaching Fellowship (UTF).

I have worked as a Production Services/Stage Technician for USU's Caine College of Arts since becoming a freshman at the university. Every workday presents unique challenges, yet I possess the skills and experiences to assist anyone seeking technical support.

I have received several certifications and awards, including: ITLS Undergraduate Scholar of the Year Award for 2024-2025, CHOICE Humanitarian 2019 Nepal Service Project, Weber County Sterling Scholar State Finalist in Computer Technology, and Solidworks CSWA Mechanical Design Certification.

My goal is to use my designing and developing skills towards projects that can help aspire improvements in people's lives. I've always been enthusiastic about the artistry and design sides of engineering, and want to make an impact with my work. The best designs are ones that encourage long-lasting positive memories and feelings.

Take a look around my website, and feel free to reach out if you have any questions!
